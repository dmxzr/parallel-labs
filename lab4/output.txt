Task 8: Comparison of different OpenMP schedules
Loop size: 2,000,000 iterations, additional work in each iteration

Schedule     | chunk    | time             | result
------------------------------------------------------------
static       | chunk 1   | time = 62.433     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
static       | chunk 8   | time = 52.816     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
static       | chunk 64  | time = 51.053     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
dynamic      | chunk 1   | time = 54.686     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
dynamic      | chunk 8   | time = 48.306     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
dynamic      | chunk 64  | time = 47.596     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
guided       | chunk 1   | time = 46.974     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
guided       | chunk 8   | time = 46.792     ms | sum = 19236637208007997
guided       | chunk 64  | time = 47.099     ms | sum = 19236637208007997

Interpretation:
- static: good for regular, balanced workloads; chunk size controls the block partitioning.
- dynamic: useful when iterations have different complexity; tasks are assigned on demand.
- guided: dynamically reduces the chunk size as execution proceeds, which is convenient for
  irregular workloads, but may add scheduling overhead.
- In a balanced synthetic example the difference may be small; in uneven workloads it becomes visible.

Conclusion
OpenMP provides flexible control over thread counts, schedules, timers and synchronization.
The correct choice of schedule depends on the type of workload and its regularity.
